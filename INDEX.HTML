About BMW

BMW stands for Bayerische Motoren Werke. It is a famous German automobile company. BMW produces luxury cars, motorcycles and electric vehicles. The company is known for its performance, quality, innovation and modern technology.

BMW History

BMW was founded in 1916 in Germany. Its headquarters are located in Munich. Initially, the company was involved in aircraft engine production. Later, BMW became one of the world's famous automobile manufacturers.
